# Employee business trips

The business trips tab shows and manages business trip records linked to the employee from inside the 360 view.

**Location:** 360 view ← **Business Trips**  
**Parent record:** `/Entities/HRM/Employee/360/{id}`

## What this tab does

- Opens the list of business trip child records linked to this employee
- Lets you review and manage trips from the employee context
- Keeps the trip linked to employee identity for follow-up with expenses and attendance when needed

This tab is the management interface for business trip records belonging to the employee. Use it to review trips linked to the current record and manage those records from here.

## How to review an employee’s business trips

1. Open `/Entities/HRM/Employee/360/{id}`
2. Go to the **Business Trips** tab
3. Review the records shown
4. Add, edit, or manage records according to your permissions from inside the tab

## Best Practices

:::tip[Link the trip to the employee who actually travels]
Do not record a trip under another employee “for convenience”. Reports and audit depend on the correct link.
:::

:::note[May appear alongside expenses]
When travel expenses exist, also review the [Expenses tab](/docs/hrm/employee-expenses) from the same 360 record.
:::

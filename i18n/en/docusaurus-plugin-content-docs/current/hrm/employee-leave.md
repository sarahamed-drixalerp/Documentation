# Employee leave

The leave tab shows and manages leave (Vacations) records linked to the employee from inside the 360 view.

**Location:** 360 view ← **Vacations**  
**Parent record:** `/Entities/HRM/Employee/360/{id}`

## What this tab does

- Opens the list of leave child records linked to this employee
- Lets you review leave requests or records and manage them from the employee context
- Keeps leave history linked to employee identity for reporting and follow-up

This tab is the management interface for leave records belonging to the employee. Use it to review leave linked to the current record and manage those records from here without inventing fields that are not shown in the interface.

## How to review an employee’s leave

1. Open `/Entities/HRM/Employee/360/{id}`
2. Go to the **Vacations** tab
3. Review the records shown
4. Add, edit, or manage records according to your permissions from inside the tab

## Best Practices

:::tip[Review leave together with attendance]
When investigating absence on a given day, open the Attendance and Vacations tabs from the same 360 record before changing any payroll figure.
:::

:::note[The tab is named Vacations in the interface]
In the system the tab appears as Vacations even if daily conversation calls the process “leave”.
:::

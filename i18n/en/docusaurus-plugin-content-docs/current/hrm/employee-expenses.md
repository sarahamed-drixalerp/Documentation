# Employee expenses

The expenses tab shows and manages employee expense records linked to the 360 record.

**Location:** 360 view ← **Expenses**  
**Parent record:** `/Entities/HRM/Employee/360/{id}`

## What this tab does

- Opens the list of expense child records linked to this employee
- Lets you review and manage employee expenses from the record context
- Keeps the expense linked to employee identity for administrative follow-up

This tab is the management interface for expense records belonging to the employee. Use it to review expenses linked to the employee and manage those records from here.

## How to review an employee’s expenses

1. Open `/Entities/HRM/Employee/360/{id}`
2. Go to the **Expenses** tab
3. Review the records shown
4. Add, edit, or manage records according to your permissions from inside the tab

## Best Practices

:::tip[Keep expenses under the correct employee record]
Any expense without a clear employee link makes later audit with payroll or business trips harder.
:::

:::warning[Review permissions before delete]
Deleting an expense record tied to financial follow-up may not be recoverable. Confirm the internal policy first.
:::

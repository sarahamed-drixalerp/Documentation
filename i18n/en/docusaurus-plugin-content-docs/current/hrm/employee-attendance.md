# Employee attendance

The attendance tab shows and manages attendance records linked to the employee from inside the 360 view, without first searching for the employee on other screens.

**Location:** 360 view ← **Attendance**  
**Parent record:** `/Entities/HRM/Employee/360/{id}`

## What this tab does

- Opens the list of attendance child records linked to this employee
- Lets you review and manage attendance records from the employee context
- Keeps the employee link clear for audit or administrative follow-up

This tab is the management interface for attendance records belonging to the employee (ListSub). Use it to review attendance linked to the current record and manage those records from here.

## How to review an employee’s attendance

1. Open `/Entities/HRM/Employee/360/{id}`
2. Go to the **Attendance** tab
3. Review the records shown for the employee
4. Add, edit, or manage records according to your permissions from inside the tab

:::note[Personal attendance in the app]
For daily attendance from the employee’s own perspective, also see the [attendance in the app](/docs/app/attendance) guide if the attendance app is enabled for your organization.
:::

## Best Practices

:::tip[Review attendance from 360 before closing a payroll run]
Any dispute about work days or overtime is best resolved from the employee record before payroll calculation.
:::

:::warning[Do not separate attendance from employee identity]
Manage records from the employee tab so the cost and reporting link stays sound.
:::

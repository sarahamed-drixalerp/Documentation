# Contacts

A **Contact** is a person linked to a customer and/or supplier in CRM. Use contacts for day-to-day communication and optional VIP promotion.

**Navigation:** Sidebar → Sales → Contacts  
**Pages:** `/Entities/CRM/Contact` · `/Entities/CRM/Contact/{id}`

There is no 360 view; the record page hosts Details, Activities, Attachments, Logs, and Insights where available. Contacts also appear as a sub-list under the customer.

## What a Contact does for your business

- Stores person identity: name, first/last, email, mobile, job title
- Links to **customer** and/or **supplier**
- Supports **Promote VIP** and DePromote
- Appears on customer 360 under Contacts

## Contact list

### Columns (key)

Name, first/last name, email, mobile, job.

### Filters

Search, type, status, customer, supplier, account manager, country.

## Required fields

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| **name** / **nameAr** | Display and search |
| **type** / **status** | Record governance |

Link **customer** and/or **supplier** as needed for the person’s role.

## Promote VIP

| Action | Function |
|---|---|
| **Promote VIP** | Marks the contact as VIP |
| **DePromote** | Removes VIP status |

## How to create a Contact

1. Open Contacts (or the Contacts tab on customer 360)
2. Create a contact and enter bilingual names, type, and status
3. Link customer and/or supplier
4. Save, then Promote VIP if the person should be flagged

## Best Practices

:::tip[Link to the right account]
Always set customer or supplier so the contact appears in the correct 360 and filters.
:::

:::note[VIP is optional]
Promote VIP only for people who need that flag in lists and follow-up; it is not required for qualification.
:::

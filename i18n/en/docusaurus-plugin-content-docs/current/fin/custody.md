# Custodies

A **Custody** records cash advances or company assets issued to an employee or department, tracking settlement and return.

## What Custodies do for your business

- Tracks employee petty cash advances and physical custodianship
- Manages custody disbursement, expense settlement, and refunds
- Links to journals upon financial clearance

**Path:** Sidebar → Finance → Custodies  
**Pages:** `/Entities/FIN/Custody` · `/new` · `/{id}`

## Custody lifecycle

1. **Request / Issuance**: Custody is created with custodian employee, amount, and purpose.
2. **Disbursement**: Custody funds or items are delivered to the employee.
3. **Settlement**: The employee submits invoices/expense receipts against the custody.
4. **Closure**: Any remaining balance is returned and the custody is closed.

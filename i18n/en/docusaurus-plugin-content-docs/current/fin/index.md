# Finance

The **Finance** module in Drixal ERP covers the chart of accounts, journals and posting, cash transfers, cost/profit centers, expenses, custody, loans, assets, commercial lookups, reports, and ZATCA e-invoicing setup.

Click **Help** in the app header on the page to open the matching guide.

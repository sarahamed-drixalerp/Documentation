# Financial Reports

Comprehensive reporting suite for balance sheets, profit & loss, trial balance, general ledger, and tax reporting.

## Key financial reports

- **Balance Sheet**: Assets, liabilities, and equity overview
- **Income Statement (P&L)**: Revenues, cost of goods, operational expenses, net profit
- **Trial Balance**: Sum of debits and credits across all active accounts
- **VAT Return Summary**: Output VAT, input VAT, and net tax liability for ZATCA filing

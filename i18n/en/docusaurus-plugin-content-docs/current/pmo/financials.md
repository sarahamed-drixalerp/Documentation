# Financials and Billing

The Financials tab connects project delivery to cash flow: when to submit claims, what has been invoiced, what has been paid to suppliers, and which purchase orders remain open.

**Location:** 360 View ← **Financials**

The four sections are:

1. Billing Plan
2. Customer Invoices (AR)
3. Supplier Invoices (AP)
4. Purchase Orders

## Billing Plan

The billing plan is the schedule of expected claims: what to invoice, when to invoice it, and its current status.

**Common types**

- Material supply
- License delivery
- Manpower

**Plan statuses**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Planned | The payment is in the plan but is not yet due |
| Due | It is time to submit the claim |
| Invoiced | An invoice has been issued |
| Submitted | The claim has been submitted to the client |
| Approved | The client has approved it |
| Collected | Payment has been received |

Link the plan to the client and to the customer invoice when it is issued.

## Customer Invoices

The sales invoice linked to the project includes the client, account manager, sales order, amount, invoiced amount, collected amount, invoice date, and due date.

Review the following:

- Were the amounts invoiced according to plan?
- Are there overdue invoices that have not been collected?
- Does the invoice currency match the project currency?

## Supplier Invoices

Supplier costs for the project include the supplier, purchase order, amount, claimed amount, invoice date, and due date.

This data contributes to the actual cost and Cost Performance Index.

## Purchase Orders

Purchase commitments linked to the project before the supplier invoice arrives include the supplier, account manager, issue date, value, budget, and planned profit.

Use purchase orders so that costs do not appear unexpectedly only when invoices arrive.

## Financial Indicators in the Project

These are calculated at the record level and appear in Analytics and the header:

- Value / budget / planned profit / actual profit
- Actual cost and remaining cost to complete
- Recognized and unrecognized revenue
- Invoiced, uninvoiced, and due amounts
- Collected and uncollected amounts
- Paid and unpaid amounts
- Planned and unplanned amounts

:::note[Some roles cannot view amounts]
If you only have operational read access, “Information hidden” may appear instead of the figures.
:::

## Best Practices

:::tip[Do not invoice from memory]
Every claim should follow this path: milestone or line item → billing plan → customer invoice. This flow protects collections when the finance and project teams are different.
:::

:::warning[Purchase orders without a limit]
An open purchase order without a clear value weakens project budget control before work begins.
:::

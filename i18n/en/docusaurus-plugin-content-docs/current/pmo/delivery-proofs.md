# Delivery Proofs

Delivery proofs are documents showing that work was delivered to and accepted by the client. Without them, billing is delayed and your position in any dispute is weakened.

**Location:** 360 View ← **Delivery Proofs**

There are three record types:

1. Delivery Note
2. Certificate of Completion (COC)
3. Team Roster

Each can be linked to a phase, milestone, contact, or customer invoice, depending on the work context.

## Delivery Note

Documents that materials, services, or a work package have been transferred from your custody to the client or site.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Issue Date | The delivery date |
| Approval Date | The date the client or consultant accepted the delivery |
| Phase / Milestone | The timeline context |
| Contact | The recipient |
| Customer Invoice | The financial link, if applicable |

Use a delivery note before submitting a claim related to a supply or partial delivery.

## Certificate of Completion (COC)

A Certificate of Completion confirms that an agreed scope—a phase, project, or major milestone—has been completed. It is often required for the final or handover payment.

Link the certificate to:

- The client
- The completed phase or milestone
- The approving party
- The resulting customer invoice

:::tip[Do not issue the handover invoice before the Certificate of Completion]
If your contract requires formal acceptance, the certificate—not a verbal conversation—is the key requirement.
:::

## Team Roster

A list of team members who worked on the project or phase. It is sometimes required for manpower contracts or security audits at the client site.

Link the roster to the phase or milestone if the contract bills for a specific team's attendance during a defined period.

## Best Practices

:::warning[Proof without an approval date]
A delivery note or certificate without an approval date remains an “internal claim,” not a client-facing record.
:::

:::note[Upload the attachment to the record itself]
Use project attachments or child-record attachments for the delivery receipt image or signed certificate copy.
:::

# Timesheet Approval

Hours logged by the team against project tasks are not included in approved cost and progress until the project manager reviews them. The **Timesheet Approval** button in the project header opens this review.

## What Approval Does

- Displays only records pending for this project
- Lets you select all records or specific rows
- Shows the employee, task, date, hours, and cost
- Approves selected records or rejects them with a written reason

## Time Record Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending Approval | Requires your decision |
| Approved | Included in cost and progress |
| Rejected — Changes Required | Returned to the employee to correct and resubmit the hours |
| Previously Reviewed | An older record whose review is complete and cannot be edited here |

## How to Approve

1. Open the project.
2. Click **Timesheet Approval**.
3. Review the list. If it is empty, there are no pending records.
4. Select the correct records.
5. Click Approve.

The screen summarizes the number of pending records, the number selected, and the total selected hours and cost.

## How to Reject

1. Select the unacceptable records.
2. Click **Reject Selected**.
3. Enter the rejection reason (required).
4. Confirm.

The reason is shown to the employee so they can correct and resubmit the hours. Do not reject records without a clear reason (“Incorrect” is not sufficient).

## How Settings Affect Hours

From [Project Settings](/docs/pmo/project-details):

- Hour increment (such as 1 hour) sets the time-entry unit
- Maximum regular and overtime hours prevent excessive daily entries

If you reject hours because an employee logged 12 hours on a day capped at 8+4, explain this in the reason.

## Best Practices

:::tip[Approve on a consistent schedule]
Weekly approval is better than accumulating a full month. Delays hold back the actual cost and CPI.
:::

:::warning[Do not approve hours for a closed or out-of-scope task]
If the task was completed two weeks ago or belongs to another project, reject the entry and redirect it.
:::

:::note[Approved days are locked]
After approval, the employee cannot edit that day. The decision is final unless an administrative correction process is used.
:::

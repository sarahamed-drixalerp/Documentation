# Management Center

The Management Center is the project's written memory: what we assumed, what constrains us, what we planned, what we decided in meetings, and what we learned.

**Location:** 360 View ← **Management Center**

| Record | Question It Answers |
|---|---|
| Assumption | What did we consider true without complete evidence? |
| Constraint | What limit can we not exceed? |
| Plan | Which document or methodology is approved for execution? |
| Minutes of Meeting | What was decided, and who will do what? |
| Lesson Learned | What should we repeat or avoid in the next project? |

## Assumptions

Common types: technical or administrative.

Common statuses: Draft / Published.

Example: “The site will be ready on March 1” or “The client will provide electricity within one week.” If an assumption proves false, convert it into a risk or issue and record a change request when needed.

## Constraints

Common types: resources, budget, and time.

A constraint is not a risk. It is a known limit from day one (a budget capped at X, delivery before Ramadan, or a limited number of engineers). Make constraints visible to the team so they do not plan beyond them.

## Plans

Approved plan documents may include a quality plan, communications plan, procurement plan, or safety plan, depending on your contract. The name and description are sufficient for classification; attach the document through Attachments.

## Minutes of Meeting (MOM)

Record every decision-making meeting—with the client or internally—using the minutes date. Minutes protect the project from “we agreed verbally.”

**Minimum content for effective minutes**

- Date and attendees
- Decisions
- Actions with an owner and date
- Open items

## Lessons Learned

Common types: technical or administrative. Statuses: Draft, then Published.

Do not postpone lessons until the end of the project. Record a lesson after each major issue is closed, then publish it so other project managers can see it.

## Best Practices

:::tip[Publish assumptions and constraints in the first week]
A new team joining in the third month needs this page more than it needs the project description.
:::

:::warning[Minutes without actions]
A meeting without an owner for every decision will be repeated the following week in the same words.
:::

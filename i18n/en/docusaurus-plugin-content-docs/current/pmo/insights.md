# Analytics and Performance Indicators

Analytics answers the question: is the project on track in terms of schedule and finances? It is available from the 360 View sidebar, the tree view in the project list, and the **PMO Dashboard**.

## Where to View Analytics

| Location | Scope |
|---|---|
| 360 View ← Analytics | A real-time view of one project |
| Project List ← Tree View | A project or group, depending on the grouping |
| PMO ← Dashboard | An overview that can be filtered by one project or all projects |

## What the Project Cards Show

### Financial Performance

- Net value
- Budget
- Recognized revenue
- Unrecognized revenue

### Schedule and Cost Indicators

| Indicator | Simplified Interpretation |
|---|---|
| **SPI** Schedule Performance Index | Greater than 1 = ahead of plan; less than 1 = behind plan |
| **CPI** Cost Performance Index | Greater than 1 = costing less than planned; less than 1 = over budget |
| Cost | Compares planned, actual, and remaining costs |

### Trends

Charts track changes over time so you do not rely only on an end-of-month snapshot.

The dashboard also adds progress and trends at a broader level, with a refresh button and the option to select a specific project or all projects.

## How to Read the Figures Accurately

- **Low SPI + normal CPI**: work is slower, but cost is under control—review resources and dependencies.
- **Low CPI + normal SPI**: the schedule is on track, but the margin is being consumed—review purchase orders, overtime, and uninvoiced change requests.
- **High unrecognized revenue**: delivery has occurred but does not match the recognition policy—review line items and completion percentages.
- **High uninvoiced amount with completed deliverables**: the billing plan or delivery proofs are incomplete.

## Access to Figures

If your policy hides monetary amounts, schedule indicators remain visible while financial cards are hidden. This is intentional.

## Best Practices

:::tip[Do not manage the project based on how the week feels]
Open Analytics before the status meeting. The SPI/CPI figures are more reliable than “we are almost finished.”
:::

:::warning[Indicators are misleading without an updated schedule]
If task progress is not updated weekly, the project may incorrectly appear ahead of or behind schedule.
:::

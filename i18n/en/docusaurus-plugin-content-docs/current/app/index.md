# My Apps

**My Apps** is your personal workspace in Drixal ERP: tasks, calendar, timesheet, services and requests, inbox, incidents, attendance, plus profile, password, and privacy policy.

## Where to Find It

From the top apps bar, the user menu, or the sidebar (depending on the item):

| Calendar | `/app/calendar` |

## How This Guide Is Organized

1. [Tasks](/docs/app/tasks) — personal kanban board
2. [Calendar](/docs/app/calendar) — tasks and vacations in one month view
3. [Timesheet](/docs/app/timesheet) — weekly hours and progress
4. [Services](/docs/app/services) — browse and submit service requests
5. [My Requests](/docs/app/my-requests) — track submitted requests
6. [Inbox](/docs/app/inbox) — pending approvals assigned to you
7. [Incidents](/docs/app/incidents) — report and track incidents
8. [Attendance](/docs/app/attendance) — daily check-in and check-out
9. [Profile](/docs/app/profile) — photo, preferences, and security links
10. [Change Password](/docs/app/change-password)
11. [Privacy Policy](/docs/app/privacy-policy)

## Best Practices

:::tip[Start with Inbox and Tasks each morning]
Review [Inbox](/docs/app/inbox) for urgent approvals, then [Tasks](/docs/app/tasks) for daily work, and complete your [Timesheet](/docs/app/timesheet) before the week ends.
:::

:::note[Permissions and statuses come from the system]
What you see in lists, statuses, and buttons depends on your data and roles. If a button is missing or a form is read-only, that is usually a status or permission rule—not a defect.
:::
